Selected Publications

Rapid movements at segment boundaries
Svensson Lundmark, M. (2023). The Journal of the Acoustical Society of America.
What creates a segment boundary in speech?
This study explores how rapid articulatory movements help shape the timing of consonants and vowels, linking speech dynamics to acoustic structure. DOI link

Segmental and Syllabic Articulations: A Descriptive Approach
Svensson Lundmark, M. & Erickson, D. (2024). Journal of Speech, Language, and Hearing Research.
How do individual speech sounds relate to the larger rhythmic structure of syllables?
This article examines the roles of the jaw, tongue, and lips in speech production and proposes a distinction between segmental and syllabic articulations. DOI link

Jaw-opening patterns and their correspondence with syllable stress patterns
Erickson, D., Svensson Lundmark, M. & Huang, T. (2026). Rhythms of Speech and Language: Physiology, Cognition, Culture, Cambridge University Press.
How is speech rhythm reflected in articulatory movement?
This chapter investigates how jaw-opening patterns relate to syllable stress, highlighting the relationship between speech rhythm and articulatory organization. DOI link
